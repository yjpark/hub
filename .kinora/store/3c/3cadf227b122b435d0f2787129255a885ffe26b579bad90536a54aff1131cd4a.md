Task: "task: peer hubs" (this ledger).

1. **Protocol** — the hub's name, the peer handshake, registry exchange,
   qualified node ids (`::` reserved and refused at registration).
2. **Calls** — route `<hub>::<node>` one hop; never forward a peer's call.
3. **Events** — subscriptions propagate one hop; remote events arrive
   unchanged.
